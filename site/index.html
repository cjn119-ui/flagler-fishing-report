    How this is decided

    The worst rule sets the status. The score weights wind 45%, rain chance 35% and offshore seas 20%. Missing inputs are excluded and remaining weights normalized. The result is then capped or floored to match the worst rule, so it stays inside its status band (Go 70+, Marginal 40–69, Skip under 40). It is a conditions read, not a catch prediction. Thresholds use unrounded inputs; displayed readings are rounded. Airport and offshore readings can differ from the beach.

      How times are picked

      Each 30-minute daylight/twilight slot is sampled at its midpoint and scored on tide movement (35%), sunrise/sunset light (25%), wind (20%) and rain chance (20%). Times are the best non-overlapping 2-hour stretches with complete forecast and tide coverage. They include up to 45 minutes of twilight. Wind shown is the maximum across each window. Tide movement is approximated between Smith Creek predictions, an inland reference that does not give ocean beach tide timing. Alerts and Skip-level forecast wind or rain withhold suggestions.
