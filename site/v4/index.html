    How this is decided

    The worst rule sets the status. The score blends wind, rain chance and offshore seas, and always stays inside its status band (Go 70+, Marginal 40–69, Skip under 40). It is a conditions read, not a catch prediction. Airport and offshore readings can differ from the beach.

      How times are picked

      Each 30-minute daylight slot is scored on tide movement (35%), sunrise/sunset light (25%), wind (20%) and rain chance (20%). Times are the best non-overlapping 2-hour stretches. Tide movement is interpolated between NOAA's predicted highs and lows.
