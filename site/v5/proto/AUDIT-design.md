# V5 prototype design audit (2026-10-06)

Reviewer: design auditor (Claude Opus 5.5). Method: built-in browser at 375 × 812 (judged against the spec's 375 × 667 fold) and 1024 × 768, light and dark, all six review states, Today and Tomorrow, all four tabs, every sheet and the scrubber, side by side with the live report. This builds on `AUDIT-deterministic.md`; its items are merged below and tagged **[det]**. Source of truth for behavior and copy: `docs/v5-spec.md` Product spec and `docs/v5-product-decisions.md`.

## Verdict

The prototype is well built and on-brand, but it doesn't pass the 10-second test yet. On a 375 × 667 phone you can see the verdict and Best bet. Targets is cut off at the fold, and Use and Confidence sit below it, which breaks the spec's fold budget: the header and control rows take 130 px where the budget is 88, the hero takes 299 px where it is 214, and Targets takes 237 px where it is 112.

The bigger problem is trust. The same fact shows up three times: "Tide timing is unavailable" appears in the hero, Confidence and Why, and on SKIP the storm reason also appears three times. Engineering copy reaches anglers ("GO threshold still provisional", "rate ratio", "hPa", "Muted:", "Scope Today"). Red is used for stale and offline as well as for SKIP. The Plan tab's bars make five outlook days look better than today's forecast. And the scrubber says "Outside windows" while Best bet says "Fish now".

Fix the P0 list below and this becomes a credible App Store-grade surface. Most fixes are CSS and copy. Nothing needs a new design language.

### Spacing and radius scale

`--s-1: 4px; --s-2: 8px; --s-3: 12px; --s-4: 16px; --s-5: 20px; --s-6: 24px; --s-8: 32px.` Replace 2/3/5/6 → 4, 10 → 8 or 12, 14 → 12 or 16, 18 → 16. The rule is that gaps inside a component are 4 or 8, card padding and gaps between cards are 12 or 16, and gaps between sections are 24.
Radii: `--r-card: 22px` (as `.card`), `--r-tile: 16px` (`.stat`, `.row`, `.bet`, `.tv-c`, rows; 18 → 16), `--r-ctl: 12px` (buttons, `.seg`, `#refresh`, `.xbtn`), `--r-pill: 999px`.
Touch: `--hit: 44px` minimum on every control except review-only ones.
